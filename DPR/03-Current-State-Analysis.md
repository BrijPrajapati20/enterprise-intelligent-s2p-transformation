# 03 — Current State Analysis  
# Existing Procurement & Integration Landscape

---

# 3.1 Introduction

NovaGlobal Consumer Products (NGCP) currently operates through a fragmented procurement and integration ecosystem distributed across multiple global regions, ERP platforms, procurement teams, and supplier networks.

The current enterprise landscape evolved organically over several years through:
- regional expansion
- business acquisitions
- decentralized procurement operations
- local ERP implementations
- plant-specific operational models

As a result, procurement processes, supplier governance, sourcing activities, integration standards, and reporting mechanisms differ significantly across regions and business units.

This section analyzes the current-state enterprise procurement landscape and identifies the major operational, technical, and governance challenges driving the transformation initiative.

---

# 3.2 Existing Enterprise Procurement Landscape

The current procurement ecosystem is decentralized across:
- APAC
- Europe
- Americas
- Middle East

Each region operates independently with:
- separate procurement processes
- different supplier onboarding practices
- disconnected sourcing activities
- regional contract governance
- multiple ERP systems

The lack of centralized procurement governance has created:
- inconsistent procurement policies
- duplicate suppliers
- fragmented spend visibility
- procurement inefficiencies

---

# 3.19 Conclusion

The existing procurement ecosystem at NGCP reflects a fragmented and operationally inefficient landscape driven by decentralized procurement processes, multiple ERP platforms, disconnected sourcing activities, inconsistent supplier governance, and limited integration standardization.

The current-state challenges significantly impact procurement efficiency, supplier visibility, operational scalability, reporting accuracy, and enterprise compliance.

To address these limitations, NGCP has initiated the Enterprise Intelligent Source-to-Pay (S2P) Transformation Program to establish a centralized, scalable, intelligent, and AI-enabled procurement ecosystem using SAP technologies and enterprise integration platforms.
