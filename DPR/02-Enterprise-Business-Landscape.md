
# 02 — Enterprise Business Landscape  
# NovaGlobal Consumer Products (NGCP)

---

# 2.1 Introduction

NovaGlobal Consumer Products (NGCP) is a multinational FMCG and manufacturing enterprise operating across multiple global regions with diversified manufacturing, procurement, warehousing, logistics, and supplier management operations.

The enterprise operates through a distributed business model involving:
- global headquarters
- regional procurement hubs
- manufacturing plants
- shared service centers
- warehouse facilities
- regional supplier ecosystems

The organization has expanded through acquisitions and regional growth initiatives over several years, resulting in multiple ERP systems, fragmented procurement operations, inconsistent supplier governance, and disconnected sourcing processes.

The Enterprise Intelligent Source-to-Pay (S2P) Transformation Program aims to unify these operations under a centralized SAP-driven procurement ecosystem.

---

# 2.2 Enterprise Operating Model

NGCP operates under a global business model where procurement governance is centrally controlled while regional procurement execution remains decentralized to support local compliance, supplier relationships, taxation requirements, and manufacturing operations.

The enterprise business structure includes:
- global procurement governance
- regional sourcing operations
- localized supplier management
- manufacturing procurement
- warehouse procurement
- operational procurement
- strategic sourcing centers

This hybrid operating model enables centralized procurement visibility while maintaining regional procurement flexibility.

# 2.13 Conclusion

The existing enterprise procurement landscape at NGCP reflects a highly distributed and fragmented operational model with multiple ERP systems, decentralized procurement governance, disconnected sourcing processes, and inconsistent supplier management practices.

The Enterprise Intelligent Source-to-Pay (S2P) Transformation Program aims to modernize this landscape through centralized procurement governance, enterprise-wide process standardization, scalable integration architecture, intelligent procurement automation, and AI-driven procurement operations.

This business landscape establishes the foundation for the enterprise procurement transformation architecture described in subsequent sections of this DPR.
